Nothing selected

Object picks a region using the palette, so more colours in the Colours tab give smaller objects. Polygon: click points, then double-click or click the first point to close. Esc cancels, Ctrl+D clears.

Brush options

Stroke

Drag on the image. Hold still to keep applying swirl, bloat and similar brushes. Pen pressure is supported. Strokes carry across the photo and every layer. Combine brushes work between the photo and the selected layer (the top layer if none is selected): erase or dissolve it to reveal the photo, reveal only in highlights or shadows, mix colours, colourise it from the photo, or bake it into the photo. Pixel, Colour and Texture brushes paint effects onto the photo and layers.

Add an image to place it on top of the photo. You can also paste or drop one.

Transparency

Drag to move (it snaps to the centre and edges; hold Alt to skip). Corners resize and the round handle rotates (Shift snaps). Arrow keys nudge, Delete removes. The eye shows or hides, the lock protects a layer, double-click a name to rename, drag rows to reorder.
